# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: June 11, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how Tate Technologies ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Fed Trades mobile application (the "App").

## Information We Handle

Fed Trades does not require an account and does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking.

### Public disclosure data

The App downloads public congressional financial disclosure data from our static data service hosted on GitHub Pages. These requests may cause GitHub to process standard network information, such as an IP address, device or browser information, and request timestamps, as necessary to deliver and secure the service. We do not use this information to build user profiles or track activity across apps or websites.

### Push notifications

If you choose to enable notifications, Apple provides the App with an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token. The App sends that token, the APNs environment, and your enabled status to our notification service hosted by Supabase. We use this information only to send notifications about newly published congressional disclosures, prevent abuse, and maintain notification delivery.

The notification service may process an IP address briefly for rate limiting and security. We do not use notification information for advertising or analytics.

You can decline notifications, disable them in the App or iOS Settings, or contact us to request deletion. When you disable notifications in the App, the App requests deletion of its token from our service. Invalid or unregistered tokens are also deleted when identified by APNs.

### Feedback and support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message. We use it only to respond, provide support, and maintain appropriate business records.

### Information stored on your device

The App stores preferences, notification status, and cached disclosure data on your device. This information supports App functionality and is not used for advertising or tracking.

## How We Use Information

We use information only to:

- provide and update congressional disclosure data;
- deliver notifications you request;
- secure, maintain, and troubleshoot the App and its services;
- respond to support requests; and
- comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## Service Providers

We use service providers to operate the App:

- [Apple](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) provides APNs and App Store distribution.
- [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) hosts the public disclosure data and this policy.
- [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) hosts the notification registration service and database.

These providers may process information on our behalf under their own privacy terms and security practices. We require service providers that access personal information to protect it consistently with this policy and applicable law.

## Retention and Deletion

Notification device tokens are retained while notifications are enabled and are deleted when we receive an opt-out request, when APNs identifies a token as invalid or unregistered, or when deletion is otherwise requested. Support emails are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or meet legal obligations. Hosting and infrastructure providers may retain request and security logs according to their own retention policies.

To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we control, email [tatetechdev@gmail.com](mailto:tatetechdev@gmail.com). We may need to verify a request before completing it.

## Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. You may also have the right to withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing completed before withdrawal.

California residents may have rights under the CCPA/CPRA, subject to applicable exceptions, including rights to know, correct, and delete personal information and to receive equal service when exercising privacy rights. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise a privacy right, contact [tatetechdev@gmail.com](mailto:tatetechdev@gmail.com).

## International Processing

Our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are made using legally recognized safeguards.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable law. We will post the updated policy at this URL and revise the effective date.
